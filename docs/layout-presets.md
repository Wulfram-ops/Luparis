# Play layouts and map templates

The Play tab is a 12-column grid of modules: the map, the character header, the check matrix, each character card (weapons, defense, spells, maneuvers, recovery, expertise, gear, inventory, masteries, notes…), initiative, the selected token, markers and zones, illustrations and GM notes.

Drag a module by its title (the map by its ⠿ grip) and resize it by its bottom-right corner. While the button is held, the modules in the way move down as a preview; releasing keeps the change, Esc cancels it. Double-click the corner to fit a module to its content. A module never ends up shorter than its content, so none needs a scrollbar.

| Layout | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| Table | Map on the left, character, turn order, weapons and defense on the right, everything else under the map |
| Combat | Turn order in a strip above the map, the character and every action (token, weapons, spells, maneuvers, defense, recovery) on the right |
| Exploration | Large map, with markers and zones, expertise, the check matrix, inventory and equipment on the right. Initiative is hidden |
| Preparation | Map editor with markers, token, illustrations, notes and initiative. GM or local play only; the character modules are hidden |

The presets follow what players praise in the common VTTs: the map stays central with a sidebar on the right, the turn order stays in sight during a fight instead of hiding behind a tab, and attacks stay one click away next to the map.

**Customize** shows or hides modules for the current layout. Each layout remembers its arrangement in this browser, per role (table, player, spectator). **Reset layout** rebuilds the layout from the preset. On narrow screens, modules stack in the layout's order and the jump buttons take you to the map, the character or the table tools.

In Edit mode, **Map templates** opens the six previews and **Generate with AI** opens the description-based generator. Set the size or generate another variation, then use **Replace the map** when ready. Undo restores the previous map.

Right-click and drag to pan the map in Play or Edit mode. Zoom with the mouse wheel or the first two keys of the bottom letter row. The third key fits the full map.

Use the **Multiplayer** tab to host or join a session. The **Rolls** panel opens at the bottom right from any tab.
